Call the Center
How can I book an appointment?

Submit a request through the website or call +1 (555) 010-0123. Our administrator will contact you, confirm the details, and suggest an available appointment time.

Which doctor should I choose?

Tell our administrator about your symptoms or goal — they will match you with the right specialist. Not sure? Start with a general consultation.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our pediatricians see children from newborns to teenagers, and several specialists offer child-friendly appointments.

Do I need to prepare for my appointment?

Most visits need no preparation. For ultrasound or lab tests the administrator will send short instructions when confirming your visit.

What should I bring?

Bring an ID, any recent test results or prescriptions, and your insurance card if you have one.

Can I have a consultation and ultrasound on the same day?

Yes — we can book a consultation and an ultrasound back-to-back so you get results and a plan in one visit.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Of course. Call us or reply to the confirmation SMS at least 2 hours before your visit to pick a new time.
