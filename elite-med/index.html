Elite Med — Medical center landing page

Demo concept of a landing page for a fictional clinic: desktop (1920 px), mobile (390 px) and 2 ad banners. Interactive HTML prototype.

About the case

Elite Med is a demo concept website for a family medical center.

Goal: create a clear path from getting to know the clinic and its services to booking an appointment.

Audience: adult patients and parents looking for medical care for themselves and their children.

Solution: a calm teal palette, warm photography, service cards, doctor profiles, FAQ answers and an accessible booking form. The case covers desktop and mobile interfaces, a UI kit and ad banners.

The clinic and data are fictional; reviews are marked as samples. The form demonstrates the booking flow without sending any data.
