Elite MedMedical Center

The Elite Med mark combines the universal medical cross with a coral heart at its centre: clinical competence on the outside, human warmth at the core. The rounded teal square echoes the soft radii of the interface, so the mark sits naturally next to buttons and cards. The wordmark is set in Manrope ExtraBold for a confident, modern feel, with a spaced “Medical Center” descriptor for clarity.
